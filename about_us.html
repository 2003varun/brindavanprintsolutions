Company Profile

We are one of the leading dealers and distributors for screen-printing materials in Bengaluru since 1991.

Brindavan Print Solutions Started in 1991 as Brindavan Screen Traders to supply various types of screen-printing raw materials in and around Bengalore.

Brindavan increased its activities as the industry grows into textile printing raw materials, signage and display raw materials & ceramic printing materials.

As the time derived now we have started one more company Brindavan Print Solutions to cater to needs of big printing houses.

Brindavan Print Solutions managed by Mr. Prabhu with more than 20 years of experience in various print solutions. The company spends a considerable amount of time in understanding the precise needs of its customers. It has strategic business alliances with leading printing ink and raw material manufacturers and offers their products at highly competitive rates.
