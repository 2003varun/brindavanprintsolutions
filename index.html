Welcome to Brindavan




The main object of company to carry on business in India and abroad as dealers, distributors, buyers, sellers, importers and exporters for all types of printing raw materials. Mainly printing inks, self advice tapes & labels, nylon & polyester bolting cloth, Screen making chemicals, textile printing chemicals, vinyl, polycarbonates, PVC, release liners, adhesives, label stocks, screen stretching and accessories. Company also provides Printing consultancy & printing solutions for various industries.

Since its inception in the 90s, Brindavan is offering a comprehensive range of high quality printing inks and materials to image-makers, The Company has a vast distribution and support services network to cater to the precise needs of its customers. Its clientele includes textile printers, display and signage printers, fascia labels & vehicle graphics, container printers, ceramic printers, UV printers and textiles transfers.

Quality is the hallmark of all products marketed by Brindavan. The Company takes every care to ensure that its sources just the right kind products. What's more, Brindavan backs its products with unmatched customer support service to ensure total customer satisfaction.

With wide range of products, Brindavan has become a one-stop solution provider for the varied needs of printers. Brindavan's vision is to constantly strive for excellence in its area of operations.
