About Brindavan Print Solutions

Over three decades of printing industry experience. One of the leading dealers and distributors of screen-printing materials in Bengaluru, serving the industry since 1991.

We began in 1991 as Brindavan Screen Traders, supplying screen-printing raw materials to printers in Bengaluru and surrounding areas. As the industry evolved, we expanded into textile printing raw materials, signage and display materials, and ceramic printing materials.

With the growing requirements of larger printing houses, we established Brindavan Print Solutions to offer a broader range of printing materials and solutions. Rather than simply supplying products, we focus on understanding what each customer needs and identifying suitable materials for their application.
